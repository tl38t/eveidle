Privacy Policy

Deep Space Idle: Frontier Era · Effective date: September 2026

Deep Space Idle: Frontier Era (the “Game”) is independently developed and published.

1. Information We Collect

The Game may generate a randomly generated anonymous player identifier and store it locally on your device. This identifier is used to identify your alliance membership and synchronize alliance-related data.

When you use the online Alliance features, the Game may send the following information to our cloud service:

  • Anonymous player identifier
  • Alliance identifier and membership information
  • Alliance construction and task data
  • Technical request information needed to operate the service

The Game does not require your real name, phone number, contacts, email address, or payment information.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information only to provide and maintain Alliance features, manage membership, process alliance tasks and construction progress, prevent duplicate or invalid requests, and maintain service stability and security.

3. Third-Party Services

Alliance data is processed through Tencent Cloud services. The cloud service provider may process standard technical information required to deliver network requests, such as request time, IP address, and service logs, in accordance with its own policies and applicable law.

4. Offline Play

The core single-player gameplay can be used offline. The Alliance features require an Internet connection.

5. Data Storage

Game save data is primarily stored locally on your device. Alliance-related data is stored by the cloud service provider for the purpose of providing Alliance features.

6. Data Requests

To ask questions about your data or request deletion of alliance-related data, contact us at kamui198629@gmail.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or its online services change. The latest version will be made available at this URL.
